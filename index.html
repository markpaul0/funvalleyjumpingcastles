Can I select more than one item?

Absolutely. Tick every item you’re considering. The team can help shape a practical mix for your event and venue.

Are prices shown online?

Published prices appear beside equipment. Fun Valley lists a R300 castle deposit. Please confirm current pricing and delivery charges before booking.

Is availability guaranteed?

No. Availability is confirmed after your enquiry, so it helps to include alternative favourites and enquire early.

What should I include in my enquiry?

Your event date, suburb, age group, guest count and chosen equipment help the team respond with useful options.
